Common questions

A keyboard shortcut clashes with another site or extension

Open the extension popup and click Change shortcuts at the bottom. This opens Chrome's own shortcut settings, where you can assign any key combination you like to the brightness commands.

The sunrise/sunset mode says location is blocked

Chrome remembers a "Block" choice and stops asking. On the location page, click the icon at the left of the address bar, set Location to Allow, reload the page, and click the button again. Your location is only used on your device to calculate sunrise and sunset. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I go below 10% brightness?

Yes. The slider covers 10%–200%, but you can type any value from 1% to 200% in the number box next to it.

Does the extension collect my data?

No. Your settings stay in your browser and nothing is sent to any server. Read the full privacy policy.
